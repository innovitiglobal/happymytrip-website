Package Overview

Experience the breathtaking beauty of Kashmir, often called "Paradise on Earth." This carefully curated 5-night, 6-day package takes you through the most stunning destinations in Kashmir - from the serene Dal Lake in Srinagar to the snow-capped peaks of Gulmarg, the lush valleys of Pahalgam, and the pristine beauty of Sonmarg.

Enjoy a unique houseboat stay on Dal Lake, take a romantic Shikara ride, experience the thrilling Gondola cable car in Gulmarg, and explore the picturesque meadows of Pahalgam. This package is perfect for honeymooners, families, and nature lovers seeking an unforgettable Himalayan experience.

Package Highlights

  • Overnight stay in a traditional Houseboat on Dal Lake
  • Romantic Shikara ride on Dal Lake at sunset
  • Gondola cable car ride in Gulmarg (Asia's highest cable car)

  • Excursion to Sonmarg - "Meadow of Gold"

  • All meals included (Breakfast & Dinner)

Detailed Itinerary

Day 1
Arrival in Srinagar - Dal Lake & Houseboat
Arrive at Srinagar Airport where our representative will greet you. Transfer to your hotel for check-in and freshen up. In the afternoon, enjoy a romantic Shikara ride on the famous Dal Lake, witnessing the floating gardens and local life. Visit the Mughal Gardens - Nishat Bagh (Garden of Pleasure) and Shalimar Bagh (Abode of Love). Evening check-in to your traditional Houseboat on Dal Lake. Enjoy dinner with a view of the serene lake. Overnight stay in Houseboat.
Day 2
Srinagar to Gulmarg (50 km / 2 hours)
After breakfast, drive to Gulmarg, known as the "Meadow of Flowers." On arrival, take the famous Gondola cable car ride (at your own cost) to Khilanmarg or Apharwat Peak for spectacular views of snow-capped mountains. Enjoy activities like horse riding, sledging (seasonal), or simply relax amidst the beautiful meadows. Visit the historic St. Mary's Church and enjoy the scenic beauty. Return to Srinagar in the evening. Overnight stay in Srinagar hotel.
Day 3
Srinagar to Pahalgam (95 km / 2.5 hours)
After breakfast, proceed to Pahalgam, the "Valley of Shepherds." En route, visit the saffron fields of Pampore and the ancient Awantipora ruins. On arrival in Pahalgam, check into your hotel. Afternoon visit to Betaab Valley (named after the Bollywood movie Betaab) and Aru Valley, known for their pristine beauty. Enjoy the scenic views of the Lidder River. Optional activities include horse riding or trekking. Overnight stay in Pahalgam.
Day 4
Pahalgam Local Sightseeing
Full day at leisure in Pahalgam. You can explore Chandanwari (starting point of Amarnath Yatra), Baisaran Valley (Mini Switzerland), or simply relax by the Lidder River. Enjoy nature walks, photography, or indulge in adventure activities like river rafting (seasonal). Visit the local market for shopping Kashmiri handicrafts, shawls, and dry fruits. Overnight stay in Pahalgam.
Day 5
Pahalgam to Srinagar via Sonmarg (180 km / 5 hours)
After breakfast, check out and drive to Sonmarg, the "Meadow of Gold." Located at an altitude of 2,800 meters, Sonmarg offers breathtaking views of glaciers and alpine meadows. Visit the Thajiwas Glacier (pony ride at own cost). Enjoy the scenic beauty and take memorable photographs. Later, drive back to Srinagar. Evening free for shopping at Lal Chowk or exploring local markets. Overnight stay in Srinagar.
Day 6
Departure from Srinagar
After breakfast, check out from the hotel. If time permits, visit the famous Hazratbal Shrine or Shankaracharya Temple for panoramic views of Srinagar. Transfer to Srinagar Airport for your onward journey with beautiful memories of Kashmir. Tour ends with sweet memories!
